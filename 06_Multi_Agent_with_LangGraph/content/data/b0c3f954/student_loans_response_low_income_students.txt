### Response Overview

- **Greeting:** Address the customer and thank them for their inquiry regarding student loans for low-income students.

### Key Sections

1. **Overview of Student Loans for Low-Income Students**  
   - Federal student loans serve as essential financial aid, particularly for low-income students.  
   - Mention key supports such as Pell Grants and subsidized loan options.

2. **Income-Based Repayment Plans (IBR)**  
   - IBR adjusts repayments based on income, beneficial for those with lower incomes relative to their debt.  
   - Helpful for individuals beginning careers in public service or facing financial hurdles.

3. **State Financial Aid Programs**  
   - States provide additional financial aid which aids low-income students.  
   - These programs can complement federal loans and reduce overall educational costs.

4. **Loan Limits and Types**  
   - Understanding the differences between subsidized and unsubsidized loans.  
   - For example, undergraduate students can receive up to $5,500 in subsidized loans during their first year.

### Advice for Low-Income Students

- Encourage students to explore federal and state aid by completing the FAFSA.  
- Recommend researching grants that do not require repayment.  
- Suggest considering careers in public service that may offer loan forgiveness.

### Closing
- Offer to provide further assistance or clarification as needed, and conclude with a polite closing and contact information.
